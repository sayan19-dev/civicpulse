Who We Are

Built for citizens.
Powered by technology.

Civic Pulse is a smart location-based platform developed at Hyderabad Institute of Technology and Management. It bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities — making civic issue reporting transparent, trackable, and efficient.

AI Verified

Every complaint is validated by AI image recognition to reduce false reports.

GPS Tagged

Issues are automatically tagged with your GPS location for accurate mapping.

Real-Time Updates

Get notified at every stage from Submitted to Resolved.
